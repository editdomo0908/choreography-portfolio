About the work

ZAJ explores the shifting boundary between external voices and inner thought: advice, reactions and repeated phrases that begin outside us and gradually continue within.

Inspired by Jill Bolte Taylor’s four-character framework, the work treats different internal modes as contrasting ways of organising attention, behaviour and movement. These states coexist, compete and transform into one another, shaping how situations are perceived and how the body responds.

Through movement, sound and language, ZAJ asks what happens when these competing voices begin to lose their power to direct us.
